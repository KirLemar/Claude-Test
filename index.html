THE RFX
EXPERIENCE

Expertly crafted by fitness and racquet professionals, RFX is a one-of-a-kind fitness experience. We blend the intensity of racquet sports with high-intensity interval training.

MOTIVATING ENVIRONMENT

Our coaches bring energy and expertise to every class, pushing you to maximize each workout while ensuring proper form and technique. A community-driven atmosphere makes every session feel like a team effort.

60 MIN SESSION

Each RFX session lasts 60 minutes—just the right amount of time to get a full-body workout without feeling rushed or overworked. Every minute is optimized for maximum results.

CERTIFIED COACHES

All RFX coaches are certified fitness and racquet sports professionals. They're trained to adapt workouts to all fitness levels while maintaining the high-energy pace that defines RFX.

STRUCTURE

01.

WARM UP (5 MINUTES)

Prepare your body with dynamic stretches and light cardio to get your heart rate up and muscles ready for action.

02.

HIIT SESSION 1 (10 MINUTES)

Intense full body circuit with movements specific to racquet sports: shuffles, lunges, and more, using elastic bands to enhance resistance.

03.

PICKLEBALL SESSION 1 (10 MINUTES)

Guided drills focusing on footwork, volleys, and dinking. Helps develop technique while keeping your heart rate elevated for continued cardio-vascular conditioning.

04.

BREAK (5 MINUTES)

A short period to hydrate, recover and prepare.

05.

HIIT SESSION 2 (10 MINUTES)

Another round of high-intensity, 30-second-on/10-second-off intervals featuring exercises like burpees, mountain climbers, and jump squats.

06.

PICKLEBALL SESSION 2 (10 MINUTES)

Doubles or singles sports activities that emphasize movement and competitive gameplay for a full-body workout.

07.

BURNOUT (5 MINUTES)

Finish strong with an upper body burnout using bands to exhaust your muscles and maximize calorie burn.

08.

COOL DOWN (5 MINUTES)

Gentle static stretches and deep breathing for full recovery and mindfulness.
